## Observations

- Anzu outperforms OSS by 6–7 pts on seen tasks, ~11 pts on unseen — OSS is systematically harder, especially for novel tasks
- `PushCoasterToMug` is a clear weak point (42% on both sims) — not a sim-side issue
- Utensil crock tasks (`PutSpatulaInUtensilCrock`, `BimanualPutSpatulaOnPlateFromUtensilCrock`) show the widest anzu↔oss gaps (17–20 pts) — crock-grasping is systematically harder in OSS sim
- Unseen avocado is surprisingly strong on Anzu (90.5%), suggesting FT transfers well even when MT base hadn't seen the task
- `PutKiwiInCenterOfTable` and `PutBananaOnSaucer` are weaker across the board — centering/saucer-placement tasks
- `BimanualPutRedBellPepperInBin` is the strongest task (96.5% anzu)
